📊 Strategy Criteria

VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern)

Price-only Minervini trend template plus contraction. Not a SEPA earnings screen.
• Close ≥ $5, ADV$ ≥ $3,000,000 (30-day average share volume × close), and market cap ≥ $5,000,000,000 (TradingView `market_cap_basic`). A missing cap does not pass
• TradingView ADR ≥ 4%
• Close > SMA50 > SMA150 > SMA200, and at least 30% above the 52-week low
• Within 25% of the 52-week high. All-time high is not the gate
• SMA200 higher than it was about a month ago, from yfinance daily bars
• RS proxy ≥ 70: percentile of 20% 1M + 40% 3M + 20% 6M + 20% 12M inside the liquid universe (price, ADV$, and market cap ≥ $5B). Not the IBD RS Rating
• Last 10 sessions' range at least 15% tighter than the range 20 sessions earlier, and 10-session volume ≤ 80% of the prior 20 sessions
Skipped in v1: earnings growth, EPS acceleration, sponsorship.

Qullamaggie

Leadership is a universe percentile, not a fixed 6-month return.
• Same price, ADV$, $5B market-cap floor, and ADR ≥ 4% floor. No ADR ceiling
• Blended 45% 1M + 35% 3M + 20% 6M performance at or above the 95th percentile of that liquid universe (top 5%)
• Within 15% of the 52-week high
• Close within 5% of the 10- or 20-day average, above the 20-day, with the 20-day rising and the 10-day not rolling over
• 15-session high-low range ≤ 15% and not more than 15% wider than the previous 15 sessions

HTF (High Tight Flag)

Pole plus a short flag. This is not a higher-time-frame filter, and there is no 6-month return cap.
• Same price, ADV$, $5B market-cap floor, and ADR ≥ 4% floor
• Pole ≥ 80% from the lowest close of the first 5 sessions of a 20–40 session thrust to that thrust's high. The thrust has to finish near its high
• The most recent 10–30 sessions are the flag. Drawdown from the pole high ≤ 25%, and the flag does not run more than 5% through the pole high
• Flag average volume ≤ 85% of pole average volume
• Close near the 10- or 20-day average and within 30% of the 52-week high

52W High

Close is within 0.1% of TradingView's 52-week high (at least 99.9% of that high). The scanner applies that test directly, then this job pages through every match and sorts by distance. It does not take the largest 1,000 names by market cap and then check the high. Liquidity is close ≥ $5, ADV$ ≥ $3,000,000, and market cap ≥ $5,000,000,000. That floor is a minimum; every near-high match at or above it is still paged in. OTC names are excluded. ADR is not required here, so quieter large caps can still show up.

52W distance and RS

Distance to the 52-week high is still the near-high gate. It is kept on the row for the 52W sort and is not drawn as a metric. All-time high is not a gate for VCP, Qullamaggie, or High Tight Flag.
RS is the 1–99 percentile proxy above. SPY's 6-month change in the header is market context, not the RS calculation.

Names, links, and sectors

The title is TradingView's company name (`description`). The paragraph under it is yfinance `longBusinessSummary` from the same info request as short interest, when Yahoo returns one. The ticker opens the TradingView chart.
Sector strength counts names on this board. There is no pinned watchlist.

Chart

Each row keeps candlesticks and volume, with the chart beside the name and metrics. Gold is the 8-day simple moving average of the close. Blue is the 20-day exponential moving average. A line is drawn only after that many daily bars exist. Distance to the 52-week high and ADR stay in the screen rules and in the row data used for sorting; they are not shown as metric cells.

Setups is the default list: VCP, Qullamaggie, High Tight Flag, and 52-week high. Squeeze is the other list: gamma squeeze, gamma wall, short fuel, and call/float. A name with both kinds of badges is in both lists. Filters and sort apply inside the active list.

Field limits

ADV$ is last close times TradingView `average_volume_30d_calc`, not a true average of daily dollar volume. Market cap is TradingView `market_cap_basic`; a missing or non-numeric value fails closed and is excluded from the liquid universe and from VCP, Qullamaggie, High Tight Flag, and the 52-week high list. SMA levels come from TradingView. SMA200 slope, the VCP contraction, the Qullamaggie pause, and the flag geometry use yfinance daily bars; a name with no usable history does not get that badge. TradingView has no 9-month performance field, so the RS proxy uses 12-month for the oldest weight. A loose 1M/3M/6M floor only limits which High Tight Flag names get a history download. It has no upper bound.

GS (Gamma Squeeze Candidate)

Short-dated CALL Vol/OI spike with near/OTM strike, estimated premium, and stock momentum context.
First Seen is scanner detection time; Last Trade is yfinance contract lastTradeDate. yfinance cannot confirm buy-at-ask, sweeps, BTO/STO, or whale intent — use UW/flow data to confirm.

Gamma Wall

Major upper/lower high-gamma strike and current pin candidate from 30DTE option chains. Wall strength is 0-100 using abs-GEX as basis points of the stock's 20D average dollar volume, then adjusted for data basis and expiry. This makes 100 much harder to reach and avoids showing a nearby low-quality strike as a wall. Basis OI uses open interest; Basis VOL is an intraday volume-gamma fallback when yfinance returns zero OI. Zero gamma is a regime boundary, not a magnet. yfinance data is a proxy and does not reveal true dealer inventory.

GEX (Net Gamma Exposure)

OI-based net gamma proxy in millions of dollars per 1% move. Calls are counted as positive GEX and puts as negative GEX. Positive net GEX is treated as stabilizing dealer-long-gamma pressure, where hedging tends to sell rallies and buy dips. Negative net GEX is treated as destabilizing dealer-short-gamma pressure, where hedging can chase moves and increase volatility. This is not observed dealer inventory; confirm with trade-side flow when available.

Short Fuel

Short Float % and days-to-cover from yfinance. High fuel = Short Float ≥ 10% or DTC ≥ 3; Very High = Short Float ≥ 20% or DTC ≥ 5.
Data can be delayed/stale, so treat it as squeeze context, not a real-time covering signal.

Call/Float

Displayed gamma contract volume × 100 ÷ float shares. This explains how large the shown contract is relative to float; it is not an independent signal and is not delta-adjusted. ≥2% is notable, ≥5% high, ≥10% extreme.
